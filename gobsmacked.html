Welcome to Gobsmacked!

Gobsmacked! is a thrilling puzzle game developed by Room One Studio. Dive into a world full of mind-bending puzzles, secrets, and adventure. Our goal is to provide a fun and engaging experience suitable for all ages, rated PEGI 12 and ESRB E10+.
